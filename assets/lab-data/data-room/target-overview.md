## The target

**Lighthouse Coastal Insurance Group, LLC** ("Lighthouse") is an independent retail insurance
agency headquartered in **Pensacola, Florida**, with satellite offices in Mobile, AL and
Panama City, FL. Founded 1987; majority-owned by founder **Warren Ketchum** (68), who has
signaled a desire to retire within three years. 94 employees, of whom 22 are producers.

- **Book:** ~70% commercial P&C (coastal hospitality, marine, construction), 18% employee
  benefits, 12% personal lines (high-value coastal homes).
- **2025 revenue:** $31.65M (see `target-financials.csv` for the 3-year P&L). 2025 EBITDA
  $10.25M (32.4% margin).
- **Growth:** total revenue up 19.9% over two years — but see the revenue-mix note below.
- **Systems:** AMS360 agency management, standalone QuickBooks for the F&I entity, no
  consolidated close process; monthly reporting is producer-built Excel.

## Questions the deal team wants answered (lab prompts start here)

- What is the **quality of Lighthouse's revenue growth** — how much of the 2023→2025 increase
  came from contingent commissions vs core commissions, and why does that matter for valuation?
- What **carrier concentration risk** does the book carry, and which single relationship would
  most affect contingent income if loss ratios deteriorated?
- What would a **coastal-catastrophe year** do to the earn-out math in the proposed term sheet?
- What integration questions should finance raise **before** close (close process, systems,
  the F&I entity's separate books)?
