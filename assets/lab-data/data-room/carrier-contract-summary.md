## Diligence observations

- **Concentration:** the top carrier represents **38% of core commissions** and an estimated
  **52% of 2025 contingent income**. The Gulfstream agreement is annually renewable and
  amendable on 90 days' notice — the single largest revenue-quality risk in the book.
- **Contingent dependence:** 2025 contingent commissions of $4.9M equal **15.5% of total
  revenue**, up from 9.3% in 2023 (`target-financials.csv`). Florida coastal property loss
  ratios drive most of it; one landfalling hurricane in the Pensacola–Panama City corridor
  would likely zero the Gulfstream and Sable River profit-shares for the year.
- **Assignability:** four carrier agreements (including Gulfstream) require **carrier consent
  on change of control**. Consent has not yet been requested.
