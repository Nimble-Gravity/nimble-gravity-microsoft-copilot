Make It Stick

A two-hour session changes what you know; the next week decides what you keep. This lesson is the retention plan: a personal system that saves and automates your best prompts, a team system that gives agent outputs a governed home, and a five-day practice plan sized for a close-week calendar.

The Analyst Agent & Capstone · ~10 min read
01 — The personal system

Stop retyping your best work

The compounding gains come from reuse. Three features turn today's wins into standing infrastructure — each one takes minutes to set up.

A prompt library

Every four-element prompt that produced something you actually used gets saved — Copilot's saved prompts, or a simple OneNote page per workflow. Your library is your personal playbook; within a month it covers most of your recurring asks.

Scheduled prompts — up to 10

Promote the winners to a schedule: the Monday digest of close-calendar mail and meetings, the daily close-week status pull, the monthly variance-commentary refresh after the flash. Ten slots per user; output lands in your chat history before you're at your desk.

Copilot Memory

Save standing preferences once — "neutral, factual close-package tone," "variances in $ and % of budget," "I cover Specialty Distribution" — and stop restating them. Review what's remembered on the Memory settings page; it's yours to prune.

02 — The team system

Give the work a shared home

Personal habits lift one teammate; shared surfaces lift the desk. Two features plus one agreement cover it.

Notebooks

A shared Notebook as the close binder

A Copilot Notebook answers only from the sources you add — load the close calendar, the current flash, the variance commentary, and the rec status, and the whole team asks grounded questions against one governed set instead of six inbox threads.

One binder, one source of truth, grounded answers for everyone on the desk.
Pages + the save rule

Living handoffs, governed landings

Copilot Pages turn chat outputs into persistent, shareable canvases — open-items lists and handoffs that stay current instead of dying in a thread. And agree as a team where agent outputs get saved: a governed folder per workflow, because agent sessions aren't e-discoverable by default.

The team agreement: no Researcher or Analyst output stays only in the chat.
03 — The one-week practice plan

Five days, one rep per day

One small, real rep a day for the week after the session — each on your own work, inside the data rules from Module 1. By Friday, every surface from the workshop has touched your actual desk.

Day 1
Rerun the chat sprint — for real
Take the find → explain → draft sprint from Module 1 and run it on a real file you're permitted to use, Work scope, "/" references. Save the winning prompts into your library.
Day 2
One Excel Copilot variance table
In a real workbook: have Copilot build one variance table or schedule, then open Show Changes and review its attributed edits — the audit habit from Module 2.
Day 3
Commission one Researcher mission
One real research question with a named deliverable — carrier context, market backdrop, an integration question. Answer its clarifying questions well; that's the craft from Module 3.
Day 4
One Analyst run you verify
Metric, dimension, grain — on real permitted data. Read the Python pane, make one in-thread correction, and save the output into a governed folder.
Day 5
Schedule two prompts
Promote this week's two best prompts to a schedule — the Monday digest and one close-calendar recurrence. That's the week's work compounding on its own.
