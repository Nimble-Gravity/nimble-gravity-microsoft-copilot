Word & PowerPoint

Finance work ends in documents: the variance memo, the MD&A section, the board deck. This lesson covers Copilot where the writing happens — Word drafts grounded in the files you name, PowerPoint decks built outline-first on approved brand kits, and the Pages and Notebooks that keep the supporting material in one governed place.

Copilot in the Apps · ~12 min read
01 — Word

First drafts grounded in your files

The unlock in Word is referencing: point Copilot at the variance table you built in Excel and the prior-month memo, and the draft starts from your numbers and your house style — not a blank page and a generic essay.

01
Draft from referenced sources
"Draft the June variance commentary using /profit-center-pnl.csv and last month's memo as the template." Copilot grounds the draft in the files you name — the Source element from Module 1, doing its heaviest lifting here.
02
It edits the document directly
As of 2026, Copilot in Word makes edits directly in the document by default — restructuring sections, tightening paragraphs — rather than proposing text in a side pane. Track changes and your own review pass remain the controls; nothing ships unread.
03
Rewrite, tone, and coaching
Select a passage and rewrite it — plainer, tighter, more neutral — or use Writing Coaching from the context menu for feedback on clarity and tone. For audit-response letters, where every adjective is a liability, the neutral-tone rewrite earns its keep.
📝
The three finance drafts to stop starting from zero

Variance memos (ground in the P&L file plus last month's memo), MD&A first drafts (ground in the flash and the prior 10-Q section), and audit-response letters (ground in the request and the support schedule). In all three, Copilot writes the first 80% — the judgment in the final 20% is still yours.

02 — PowerPoint

Decks that start from the narrative

A board deck is an argument, not a stack of slides. Narrative Builder works the way a good deck review does — agree on the outline first, then build — and admin-approved brand kits mean the output starts in the house template, not a template you'll spend an hour fixing.

Narrative Builder

Outline first, slides second

Describe the deck — "quarterly finance review: EBITDAC bridge, organic walk, integration-cost update" — and Narrative Builder proposes a topic outline you edit and reorder before any slide exists. Approve the storyline and it drafts the deck, grounded in the SharePoint and OneDrive files you reference.

Fixing an outline takes minutes; fixing a finished forty-slide deck takes a weekend.
Brand kits

On-brand by default

Admin-approved brand kits (June 2026) let IT publish the corporate template — fonts, colors, layouts — so every Copilot-generated deck starts on-brand. The reformat-the-whole-deck hour before a board meeting comes off the calendar.

Ground the numbers slides in the same workbook the memo used — one source, one story, no version drift.
03 — Pages & Notebooks

A home for the working material

Two quieter surfaces round out the document story: Pages, for turning a good chat output into something the team can edit together, and Notebooks — GA July 2026 — for a workspace that answers only from the sources you put in it.

Copilot Pages

A persistent, shareable canvas made from chat outputs. When Copilot produces a strong variance summary, send it to a Page — now it's a living document teammates can edit and build on, instead of an answer buried in one person's chat history.

Copilot Notebooks

A grounded workspace that answers only from the sources you add — files, pages, links — with audio overviews on top. No drift into the wider Graph or the web: if it's not in the notebook, it's not in the answer.

The close-binder pattern

Build a notebook per close: the flash, the variance commentary, the rec exceptions, the open-items list. All month, "what did we say about the June contingent true-up?" gets answered from the binder's actual contents — a scope reviewers can reason about.
