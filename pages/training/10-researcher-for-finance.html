Researcher for Finance

The Lighthouse mission is a rehearsal for the real desk. This finance org supports a company that closed 43 acquisitions in 2025 and is integrating the largest deal in its history — research-heavy, multi-source, deadline-driven work in three recurring beats. This lesson maps Researcher missions onto each one, so you leave with briefs you could commission next week.

The Researcher Agent · ~15 min read
01 — The deal engine

Missions for a serial acquirer

Forty-three deals closed in 2025, and the largest acquisition in company history mid-integration — every one of them lands document-heavy research on finance's desk. These are the missions where Researcher carries the first draft, so the team's judgment goes into review and decisions instead of collection.

01
Target & data-room synthesis
Exactly the Lighthouse pattern at real scale: synthesize a data room's financials, commission schedules, and carrier contracts into a cited diligence brief that answers the deal team's named questions.
02
Market briefs for earn-out exposure
Earn-out liabilities are remeasured at fair value every quarter, and contingent exposure moves with the market. A web-scoped mission on the relevant market's conditions gives the remeasurement memo a documented, cited backdrop.
03
Integration-question packs
Before an acquired agency's GL comes into consolidation: a mission across the deal file to surface close-process gaps, system differences, and policy mismatches — a ready question list for the integration kickoff.
04
Purchase-accounting precedent scans
How have peers disclosed similar intangibles, goodwill allocations, or earn-out structures? A mission across public filings returns a precedent scan with citations — the starting point for the accounting memo, not the memo itself.
02 — The carrier economics beat

Research behind the estimates

Contingent commissions turn on carrier underwriting results, and float income turns on rates — estimate-driven numbers that need a documented story. Researcher's work-plus-web reach fits this beat unusually well: the inputs live outside the tenant, the context lives inside it.

Contingents

The market behind the estimate

Contingent-commission estimates rest on views of carrier profitability. A quarterly mission on the property-cat rate environment and published carrier results — scoped to the carriers and lines that matter — gives the estimate a cited market backdrop instead of a hallway consensus.

The estimate stays yours; Researcher documents the world it lives in.
The float

Rate research for fiduciary cash

Investment income on fiduciary cash moves with short-term rates. A web-scoped mission on the rate outlook and how comparable holders position short-duration cash feeds the sensitivity analysis — and the forecast narrative that goes with it.

Pair it with Analyst (Module 4) when the question turns from "what's happening" to "what does it do to our number."
📊
The peer benchmark scan

A recurring mission worth its query: how do peer brokers disclose and discuss contingent and supplemental commissions in their filings and earnings materials? A cited benchmark scan sharpens both the internal estimate discussion and the external disclosure drafting.

03 — The reporting calendar

10-Q, 10-K, and the questions before they're asked

Every quarter ends in documents — and every document starts with research someone has to gather. Three missions that slot straight into the external-reporting cycle, each producing a cited input that the team then shapes and owns.

MD&A background

A mission across the quarter's internal reporting plus the external environment — market conditions, industry developments — produces the cited background pack the MD&A drafter starts from. Drafting support, not drafting: the words stay the team's.

Disclosure benchmarking

Before finalizing a judgment-heavy disclosure — earn-outs, contingents, segment changes — a scan of how peers handle the same disclosure gives the team a defensible view of market practice, with the sources attached.

Audit-committee question prep

A mission on the quarter's themes — the integration, the estimates that moved, what peers were asked — produces a likely-questions pack with suggested support for each answer. Walking in prepared, at the cost of one query.

🗓️
Put missions on the close calendar

The best finance missions recur. If a research pack is useful this quarter, it's useful every quarter — add "commission the mission" to the close checklist a week before you need the output, so the 10–45 minute run time never sits on the critical path.
