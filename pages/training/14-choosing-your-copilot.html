Module 4 · Lesson 3 of 5

Choosing Your Copilot

Four modules in, you have four capable tools — and the failure mode flips from "can't get a good answer" to "asked the wrong tool." This lesson is the decision framework: four rules that route any finance task to the right surface, the budget and governance overlay that disciplines the choice, and the map back to the work on your own desk.

The Analyst Agent & Capstone · ~12 min read
01 — The framework

Four rules, one routing

Read the task, not the tool. Each rule keys on what the task actually needs — where the truth lives, where the output lands, and how much computation stands between the two.

01
Quick fact or draft → Copilot Chat
You know which sources hold the answer and you need it found, summarized, or drafted — Work IQ, "/" references, four-element prompt, citation checked before you trust it. "What did we agree on the close calendar?" "Draft the variance paragraph from this thread."
02
Edits in the open file → Copilot in the app
The deliverable is the workbook, document, or deck you have open. Excel, Word, and PowerPoint Copilot act natively — with Excel's change attribution when auditability matters. The answer belongs inside the file.
03
Cited multi-source research → Researcher
The question spans work data and the web, and the deliverable is a structured, source-cited report — diligence briefs, market context, carrier research. Brief it, answer its clarifying questions, let it run.
04
Multi-file computation → Analyst
The answer requires calculating across several files — statistics, trend analysis, forecasting — and you want the method visible. Name the metric, dimension, and grain; read the Python; carry the findings back into your governed files.
02 — The overlay

Budget and governance shape the choice

The routing rules say what a task needs; three operating facts say what the choice costs and what it obliges you to do afterward. Advanced users carry both.

01
Spend the pool on missions
Researcher and Analyst share 25 queries per user per month. That's roughly one agent mission per working day — spend it on work with a named deliverable, not curiosities. If Chat can answer it, Chat should.
02
Checkpoint, again: save agent results as governed artifacts
This is the Module 1 checkpoint, back for a reason: Copilot chat history is eDiscovery-searchable via Purview — but agent session content is not e-discoverable by default. The rule, not a suggestion: anything that informs a filing, a memo, or a close package gets saved into a governed OneDrive or SharePoint location, every time — whichever of the four tools produced it.
03
Our data doesn't train the models
Prompts, responses, and Graph data are not used to train the foundation models — including the OpenAI and Anthropic models under the hood. Tool choice never changes that; it's the floor under all four rules.
💳
If a colleague isn't licensed

Unlicensed teammates can still reach agents pay-as-you-go via Copilot Credits ($0.01/credit, admin-budgeted) — worth knowing when you hand a workflow to a partner team.

03 — Map it to the desk

The framework on real B&B finance work

Run the rules over the three workloads this finance org actually carries and the routing writes itself — most jobs are a relay between tools, not a single pick.

Close & consolidation

Variance commentary and budget-vs-actuals packs at profit-center grain → Analyst finds and quantifies the trend, Excel Copilot works the schedules in the workbook, Chat drafts the narrative. Trust recs and PBC support → Excel Copilot, with the Finance agent's reconciliation as the specialist option.

The deal engine

Data-room review, target research, MD&A first drafts, board-deck prep → Researcher carries the cited diligence brief, Word and PowerPoint Copilot turn it into documents. Earn-out remeasurement and purchase-accounting math → Analyst.

Carrier economics

Contingent-commission estimates against carrier loss ratios, cash-flow and interest forecasting, float-income sensitivity → Analyst is the center of gravity, with recurring digests handed to scheduled prompts once the prompt has earned its keep.
