Pre-work & Follow-through

One two-hour session means the prep is small and the follow-through matters. Fifteen minutes before the session gets your machine ready; a short challenge per module afterward is what turns two hours into a lasting habit.

15 min before · one challenge per module after
Before the session · ~15 minutes

The only true pre-work

Do this the day before. The session is fully hands-on from minute ten — if your Copilot app and lab files are ready, you spend the room time working, not troubleshooting.

1 · Confirm your access

Sign in to the Microsoft 365 Copilot app with your B&B account. Confirm you see the Work tab and the Agents list with Researcher and Analyst in it. If either is missing, flag it to the program team before the session — don't wait until you're in the room.

2 · Stage the lab files

Copy the lab files from the resource library into a folder named Copilot Workshop in your OneDrive. They're synthetic finance files built for this program — the copy needs a little time to index so Copilot can ground on them in the room.

3 · Read, acknowledge, poll

Read Why Copilot and the Rules of the Road (acknowledge at the bottom), then take the two-minute maturity poll. Optional if you have ten more minutes: skim Lesson 1.

After the session · Module 2 follow-through

Take the app skills to real work

Run The Variance Vault

The take-home challenge: The Variance Vault — a prompting-and-agents gauntlet on the workshop's synthetic finance data. Solo or with a teammate, about 40 minutes; everything you need is in your Copilot Workshop folder.

One real Excel variance table

Build one budget-vs-actuals variance table with Excel Copilot on real data you're permitted to use — your own workbook, per the Rules of the Road. Use Plan with Copilot first, then check its work in Show Changes. Depth in Lesson 4.

After the session · Module 3 follow-through

Commission a real Researcher mission

One real mission

Within a week, brief Researcher on one genuine piece of your work — a carrier's results ahead of a contingent estimate, background on an acquisition target market, a rate-environment read for a forecast. Use the mission-brief pattern from Lesson 9 and engage its clarifying questions before you let it run.

Review like it's a first draft

When the report lands, check the citations against sources and save the output into a governed file — agent sessions aren't e-discoverable by default, so the saved file is the record. Ideas for what to commission are in Lesson 10.

After the session · Module 4 follow-through

The Close Room, the practice plan, the certificate

Complete The Close Room

Run The Close Room with your team — a month-end close fire drill using Researcher and Analyst on the synthetic data, ending in a close-package one-pager. It's the capstone briefed at 1:48 in the session; block 40 minutes together in week one.

Practice plan + knowledge checks

Work the one-week practice plan — one small real task per day. Then pass the four knowledge checks (at the end of each module's lab lesson) to earn the workshop certificate on My Progress.
