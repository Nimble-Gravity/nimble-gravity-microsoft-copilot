Wiring these up

Both buttons are placeholders. Build the two forms in Brown & Brown's Microsoft 365 tenant (already governed, no new tooling), then paste each form's link in place of the placeholder above.

01
Confidence per module (1–5)
Four quick ratings — Foundations & Chat, Copilot in the Apps, Researcher, Analyst — so the facilitator sees exactly which 25-minute block needs rework.
02
One thing you'll use this week
A single open text field. Specific answers ("the variance-commentary prompt") signal transfer; vague ones ("prompting better") signal the labs need sharpening.
03
Blockers
What got in the way — licensing, tenant settings, a file that wouldn't ground, time. Blockers route to IT or the facilitator, not into a spreadsheet nobody reads.
04
NPS
"How likely are you to recommend this session to another finance teammate?" (0–10). One comparable number across cohorts. Results roll into the readout in the facilitator guide.
