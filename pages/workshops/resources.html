Lab data

Synthetic finance files

Every lab runs on these files — synthetic, Copilot-safe, and shaped like brokerage finance data but entirely fake. During pre-work, copy them into a folder named Copilot Workshop in your OneDrive so Copilot can ground in them. Never put real client, carrier, or deal data into a workshop exercise.
