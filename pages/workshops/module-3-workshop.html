Stage 2 · In-room content · what you'll leave with

Three outcomes for this module

Concrete, not conceptual — you finish the module having actually done each of these.

You've reviewed a real report

Your Project Lighthouse run, opened and put through the reviewer's pass: four questions answered, three citations spot-checked, work vs web claims separated, gaps noted.

You own the briefing craft

Objective, scope & sources, deliverable spec, audience — plus the clarifying-phase habit that Microsoft calls the #1 Researcher best practice, seen against the room's own runs.

You've named a real mission

One Researcher mission from your actual desk — deal file, estimate backdrop, disclosure benchmark, or committee prep — drafted as a one-sentence brief to commission this month.
