Participant FAQ
01 — The honest questions

Let's start with the real ones

Is this replacing analysts?
No. Copilot drafts; you review, decide, and sign off — the estimates, the accounting judgments, and the accountability stay with teammates. This program is about capacity: the finance org is absorbing the largest integration in company history, and Copilot takes the drafting load so your time goes to the judgment calls. Verification is the skill we're investing in. See our straight answer on jobs.
Do I have to use it?
Your manager will confirm the expectation for your team. Either way, the ask is small and concrete: 15 minutes of pre-work, one two-hour hands-on session, and an honest week of practice afterward. You already have the license — the session just teaches you to use it at an advanced level.
What if Copilot is wrong?
Expect it sometimes — that's why every output is a draft you verify against source before it's used. Check figures against the workbook or filing, check citations in Researcher reports, and watch Analyst's visible Python to confirm the method. Catching and correcting is part of the workflow, not a failure of it — your name goes on the workpaper, so the review is yours.
02 — Your data & what's allowed

Safety and the ground rules

What can Copilot actually see?
Only what you already have permission to open — your mail, meetings, chats, and files, plus content shared with you, through Microsoft Graph. It respects identity, sharing settings, and Purview sensitivity labels. It can't read another team's folder just because you asked, and a teammate without access to a file can't surface it through Copilot either.
Does it train on our data?
No. Microsoft's commitment (current as of July 2026): prompts, responses, and Graph data are not used to train the foundation models — including the OpenAI and Anthropic models running under the hood. Your close commentary isn't teaching anyone's model.
Are my Copilot chats discoverable?
Yes — Copilot chat history is retained and eDiscovery-searchable via Purview, like email. The nuance that matters for finance: Researcher and Analyst session content is not e-discoverable by default (as of July 2026) — admins see usage counts, not content. So if an agent's output matters, save it into a governed file (OneDrive/SharePoint) so there's a record. It's ground rule 4 in the Rules of the Road.
Can I use it on client data?
Per the Rules of the Road. The short version: content you're permitted to open and genuinely need for the task is generally fine on Work scope; client PII/PHI beyond need, carrier-confidential terms outside your scope, and deal MNPI in web-scoped prompts stay out. And the workshop labs use only the synthetic files — never real client or deal data. When in doubt, leave it out and ask.
03 — Getting it working

The practical stuff

I don't see Researcher or Analyst in my app.
The agents live in the Agents list in the Microsoft 365 Copilot app (and via @-mention in Copilot Chat), but they have to be enabled by your admin. If they're missing, flag it to the program team before the session — this is exactly what the pre-work access check is for.
What's the 25-query pool I keep hearing about?
Researcher and Analyst share a pool of 25 combined queries per user per month (as of July 2026; resets on the 1st). That's why the session teaches you to brief agents well — a well-scoped mission with the clarifying questions answered is one query well spent; five vague retries are five wasted. Regular Copilot Chat and Copilot in the apps don't draw from this pool.
Why did my Researcher run take 30 minutes?
That's normal for a complex brief. Simple runs finish in under 5 minutes; deep multi-source missions take 10–45. It's why the session has you commission your run in Module 1 so it lands by Module 3 — and why, on real work, you kick off a Researcher mission and get on with something else rather than watching it.
How many scheduled prompts can I set up?
Up to 10 per user (as of July 2026). Each runs on its schedule and the output lands in your chat history — good for a Monday close-status digest or a recurring variance summary. Lesson 2 covers the setup; spend the ten on recurring asks you'd otherwise rebuild by hand every week.
Do the lab files really need to be in OneDrive?
Yes. Copilot grounds on files through Microsoft Graph, so the synthetic lab files must live in your OneDrive (the Copilot Workshop folder from the pre-work) — files sitting on your desktop are invisible to it. Copy them the day before; new files need a little time to index.
04 — After the session

The certificate, more prompts, and help

How do I get the certificate?
Pass the four knowledge checks — one at the end of each module's lab lesson — and the certificate appears on My Progress. It's a personal record on your device, a marker that you finished, not an HR or compliance record. Most people knock the quizzes out during the one-week practice plan.
Where do I get more prompts?
Start with the cheat sheet — the session's prompts and patterns on one printable page — and the prompt gallery in the resource library. As your team lands prompts that work, your manager routes them into the shared prompt library so one teammate's win becomes everyone's starting point.
Who do I ask after the session ends?
Your team's Copilot champion, the program team, or the Copilot channel — set the real contacts here. The resource library keeps the lab files, cheat sheet, and reference links in one place.
