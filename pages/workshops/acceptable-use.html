01 — What goes in, and what doesn't

Mind the data

The most important rule. Copilot only sees what you already have permission to open — but permission isn't the same as appropriateness. When in doubt, leave it out and ask. These examples are a starting point — map them to B&B's data-classification tiers before applying Copilot to real client work.

Generally OK

Workshop data and your own work product

The workshop's synthetic lab files (built for this program — B&B-shaped but fake), your own drafts, notes, and working documents, and content you already have permission to open in the tenant and would be comfortable citing in your own workpaper. Always with a teammate reviewing the output.

The labs use synthetic data on purpose — practice freely there.
Do not put in

Data beyond your need or your scope

Client PII or PHI beyond what the task needs; carrier-confidential terms outside your permission scope; anything export-controlled; M&A material nonpublic information in web-scoped prompts (Work scope only, and only per policy); and never real client or deal data in the workshop labs — synthetic files only.

If you're unsure whether data qualifies, stop and ask.
02 — The ground rules

Five things that always hold

01
You own what you sign
Copilot drafts; you review, correct, and sign off. A variance narrative, a memo, an email — once your name is on it, it's yours, not Copilot's. Nothing goes out the door unreviewed.
02
Verify numbers against source
Every figure Copilot produces gets checked against the source file before it's used — the workbook, the statement, the filing. In Excel, use Show Changes to see exactly which cells Copilot touched.
03
Scope Work vs Web deliberately
Pick the grounding per question: Work for anything about our numbers, files, and people; Web for markets and outside news. Sensitive internal topics — deals especially — stay out of web-scoped prompts.
04
Save agent outputs to governed files
Copilot chat history is retained and eDiscovery-searchable — but Researcher and Analyst session content is not e-discoverable by default (as of July 2026). If an agent's output matters, save it into a governed file so there's a record.
05
Ask when unsure
No penalty for asking — ever. Questions go to [the program owner / your manager / the Copilot champions channel] — set the real contact here.
