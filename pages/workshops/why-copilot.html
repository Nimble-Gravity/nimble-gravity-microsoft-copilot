01 — The case

The workload is real — and so is the capacity

This isn't a technology pitch. It's a math problem: the finance org is absorbing the largest integration in company history while running the same close it always has — and the tools to absorb that workload are already licensed and sitting in your apps.

01
The integration workload is here
Accession — the largest deal in company history — closed in August 2025, on top of 43 deals closed in 2025. Every one of them means purchase accounting, GL onboarding, recast baselines, and integration questions landing on this team.
02
The close hasn't gotten smaller
Hundreds of decentralized profit centers still consolidate into two segments every month — variance narratives, budget-vs-actuals commentary, fiduciary cash recs, organic-revenue schedules. Copilot is capacity for exactly this document-heavy, deadline-driven work.
03
You already own the tools
Every attendee has a full M365 Copilot license — Chat, Copilot in Excel, Word, Outlook, Teams, and the Researcher and Analyst agents. Most teams use a fraction of it. This session is about using what we're already paying for, at an advanced level.
02 — What's in it for you

The payoff is different by desk

This isn't one benefit for everyone — it's a specific one for the work on your desk this month.

Close & consolidation

First drafts of variance narratives across profit centers, exception summaries on the fiduciary cash rec, organic-revenue exclusion schedules, and audit PBC support — the monthly written explanations that eat your close week.

FP&A

Budget-vs-actuals commentary packs at profit-center grain, EBITDAC bridges and organic walks for board decks, expense and synergy analysis that isolates integration costs — Analyst and Excel Copilot doing the assembly while you own the story.

Treasury

Cash-flow and interest forecasting against a $7.8B debt stack, rate-sensitivity reads on fiduciary-cash float income, and scheduled prompts that turn recurring digests into a standing order instead of a Friday scramble.

Deal support

Researcher carrying the first pass on data-room review and target research; Analyst on earn-out tracking and purchase-accounting support; Word and PowerPoint Copilot turning it into the memo and the deck.

03 — Let's be direct

Copilot drafts; you decide

The judgment stays with teammates

Every output is a first draft that a teammate reviews, corrects, and signs off. The estimates, the accounting judgments, the relationships with auditors and operating leaders — those stay with you. Nothing in this program changes who owns the numbers.

Your name goes on the workpaper, not Copilot's.
Verification is the job

The skill we're building is review

Advanced Copilot use is capacity for absorbing the integration workload — not a headcount conversation. The teammates who thrive are the ones who learn to brief these tools well and verify their output sharply. That's exactly what the session teaches.

Check the numbers against source, every time. That habit is the whole game.
