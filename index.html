What You'll Be Able to Do

From good prompts to working agents

Everyone in the room already has Copilot. This session is about using it like an analyst: grounding it in your work data, driving it inside the apps where finance actually lives, and delegating real research and analysis to the Researcher and Analyst agents — with the judgment to pick the right tool every time.

Who it's for

Brown & Brown finance teammates — accounting, FP&A, treasury, and the teams supporting close, consolidation, and the deal pipeline. Every attendee has an M365 Copilot license and a laptop; every exercise is hands-on.

What you leave with

A prompt discipline you'll actually reuse, a close-package built with Copilot in the apps, a cited Researcher report you commissioned in the room, an Analyst run you verified line by line — and take-home challenges to make it stick.
